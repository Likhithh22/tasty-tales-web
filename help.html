How can we help?

Find answers to the most common questions.

How do I place an order?

Go to the Menu page, select your favourite food and add it to your cart. You can then review your order and proceed to checkout.

How can I search for a food item?

Use the search box on the Home or Menu page to search for dishes such as Biryani, Pizza, Burger or Paneer.

How can I cancel my order?

If you need to cancel your order, please contact Tasty Tales support as soon as possible.

How long does delivery take?

Normal delivery generally takes approximately 30–45 minutes depending on your location and order.

What if my food is missing?

Contact our support team and provide your order details. Our team will help resolve the issue.

Can I modify my order?

Order modifications may be possible before your food enters preparation. Please contact us immediately.

What payment methods are available?

Depending on your restaurant setup, payment options can include UPI, debit cards, credit cards and cash.

I have another problem. What should I do?

Contact Tasty Tales support and explain your problem. Our team will help you as quickly as possible.
